Consumer Shopping Behavior Analysis

Project Overview :

This project analyzes consumer shopping behavior to identify purchasing trends, customer preferences, and factors influencing purchase and repeat purchase behavior.

The analysis focuses on customer demographics, product categories, discounts, product reviews, seasonal trends, payment preferences, and online vs. offline shopping channels.

The goal is to transform raw consumer shopping data into meaningful business insights that can help improve customer engagement, marketing strategies, and product decisions.

Business Objective

The main objective is to answer:

How can a retail company leverage consumer shopping data to identify trends, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies?**

Key Analysis Areas

* Customer demographics and purchasing behavior
* Product category performance
* Online vs. offline shopping behavior
* Impact of discounts on purchasing patterns
* Relationship between product ratings and purchasing behavior
* Seasonal shopping trends
* Customer payment preferences
* Purchase and repeat purchase behavior
* Customer segmentation and key purchasing patterns

Tools & Technologies

Python– Data cleaning, preprocessing, and exploratory data analysis
Pandas & NumPy** – Data manipulation and analysis
Matplotlib & Seaborn** – Data visualization
SQL / MySQL** – Data querying and business analysis
Power BI** – Interactive dashboard and business reporting

Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive view of:

* Overall purchase trends
* Customer demographics
* Category-wise performance
* Online vs. offline purchases

* Seasonal trends
* Payment method preferences
* Customer purchasing behavior

 Business Insights

The analysis helps identify patterns in consumer purchasing behavior and provides data-driven insights that can support:

* Targeted marketing campaigns
* Customer engagement strategies
* Product promotion decisions
* Seasonal marketing planning
* Customer retention initiatives
* Sales and product strategy optimization
